Controls

  • W A S D move   Shift sprint   Space jump
  • Left click use tool, pick, harvest, attack
  • Right click place, plant, water, wear
  • Right-drag or Q E turn and tilt the view   R reset   Wheel zoom
  • 1–9 hotbar (drag a slot onto another to swap)
  • Tab inventory and crafting   M world map   K character sheet (level, stats, skills)
  • C eat   X drink at a pond or from a flask
  • F use bed, chest, campfire, workbench; board or leave a boat or submarine
  • In deep water: Shift dives, Space swims up
  • With a glider held: jump from a height and hold Space
  • Esc pause

Touch

Move with the thumbstick. Tap terrain to aim, then hold Tool or tap Place. Drag with two fingers to turn, or pinch to zoom. Run dives and Jump surfaces.

Gamepad

Left stick moves, right stick aims. Hold the right stick button to orbit. RT uses tools, B places, X interacts, A jumps and LT sprints or dives. Bumpers select a slot. Y opens your pack, View opens the map and Start pauses. D-pad up eats and down drinks.

In menus, D-pad up and down move focus. Left and right adjust settings. A selects and B returns.

Keys can be changed under Settings.
