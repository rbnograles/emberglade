Emberglade

A cozy voxel survival game

  • W A S D move   Space jump up ledges   Shift sprint   Wheel zoom
  • Right-drag or Q E turn the view, tilt down low to see the sky   R reset view
  • Left click use tool, pick, harvest, attack night creatures
  • Right click place, plant, water, wear armor   C eat   X drink at a pond or from a flask
  • 1–9 hotbar (drag a slot onto another to swap)   Tab inventory and crafting
  • F use bed, chest, campfire, workbench   Esc pause
